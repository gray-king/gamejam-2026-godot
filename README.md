## STORY
The date is 8th November 2026, in Bristol, England. After years of research, Ratbert has finally finished his greatest creation yet: his time machine!
Excitedly, he plugs in a date, and is flung through time...

The date is 8th November 1886, in Bristol, England, and Ratbert is dismayed to find essential pieces of his time machine are missing.
You must collect all the pieces in order to return back home!

## CONTROLS
<img width="387" height="220" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f8d65790-928d-4140-b1f7-0d094ecb2e85" />

Use the arrows keys to move. When encountering cat enemies, press the DOWN arrow to avoid its claws and press SPACE to attack its tail (its weak point)
